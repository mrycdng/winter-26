snow day

it was a snow day today so my roommates & i spent it holed up in our dorm cooking. we made pasta & miso soup & eggs & mashed potato & baba ganoush & even baked baguettes.

it was one of the best days i’ve had in a while: it’s freezing outside but in this little kitchen i’m filled with so much warmth & joy.

memory is such a fragile thing. will u hold onto this day for me?
